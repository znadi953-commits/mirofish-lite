Learning & calibration: make predictions better with real results

Every run is saved in this browser. After you see what really happened (replies, sign-ups, sales), enter it next to the run. The tool compares prediction with reality and adjusts later projections for the same product. This is calibration, not retraining the AI: it needs real results and stays low-confidence until you have several.
