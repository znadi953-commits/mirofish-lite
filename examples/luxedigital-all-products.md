# LUXE DIGITAL — Product Intelligence Seed
## Purpose
This document is a structured seed for MiroFish simulations about LUXE DIGITAL. It consolidates the supplied product, customer, pain-point, positioning, buyer, and channel information. It should be treated as the factual seed supplied for the simulation; predictions and scenarios should be generated by the simulation rather than presented as established facts.

## Company/Product Scope
LUXE DIGITAL is a new online store of premium digital ebooks (French-language PDF guides) about AI, business, and income generation, aimed at an international French-speaking audience (France, Belgium, Switzerland, Canada, Morocco, North/West Africa) with prices displayed in EUR. Branding: "LUXE DIGITAL" with the tagline "Produits digitaux premium IA & Business — 2026". Store is a professional dark/gold themed website hosted free on GitHub Pages. Checkout is handled by Payhip (primary) and Gumroad (secondary). No advertising budget: traffic is planned through free channels (TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Pinterest, LinkedIn, Facebook groups, SEO blog posts).

## Bundle
PACK COMPLET 16 Ebooks — 230 EUR (instead of 383 EUR): all 16 PDFs in one zip. Positioning: best value for serious buyers, 40% saving.

## Pricing Strategy
Single ebooks: 14–35 EUR (entry 14, core 22–27, premium 29–35). Bundle 230 EUR. Perceived as premium-positioned but affordable. Competitors sell similar ebooks at 7–27 EUR on Gumroad/Etsy; LUXE DIGITAL differentiates with professional design, bonus checklists, and a luxury brand feel.

## Ideal customer profile
French-speaking digital beginners and aspiring entrepreneurs: employees wanting a side income, students, freelancers wanting to scale, content creators, and North/West African digital entrepreneurs. They are price-sensitive, want step-by-step actionable guides in French, are excited by AI opportunity but overwhelmed, and want to start an online business without large investment. Secondary: Moroccan and francophone diaspora buyers interested in remote income in EUR.

## Pain signals
- Want extra income but have no idea what to sell online
- See AI changing jobs and fear being left behind
- English-only resources are a barrier
- No budget for ads, expensive courses, or mentors
- Scams and "get rich quick" content make them distrustful
- Overwhelmed by the number of AI tools, don't know where to start
- Want concrete step-by-step plans, not theory
- Payment friction: prefer well-known checkout (PayPal/cards)

## Key buyers/influencers
The buyer themselves (self-serve impulse purchase 14–29 EUR), influenced by TikTok/Instagram creators, Facebook groups about side hustles ("argent en ligne", "freelance Maroc"), and YouTube tutorials.

## Core positioning from supplied material
Position LUXE DIGITAL as premium French "AI & Business" playbooks that give a shortcut to online income. Rational hook: professional, complete, actionable step-by-step guides with bonuses. Emotional hook: luxury feeling of investing in yourself, "level up" identity, proven modern methods. Desired outcome: launch a freelance/automation/creator/AI-agency business fast.
